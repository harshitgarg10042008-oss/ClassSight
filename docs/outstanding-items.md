# ClassSight Outstanding Items

## High priority — review-queue safety at classroom scale

The corrected Stage A ranked-distance investigation did not reproduce the earlier apparent 3-of-4 identity confusion; that result came from a crop-to-face ground-truth artifact in the initial follow-up harness. Nevertheless, the safety assumption that absolute distance below `0.6` reliably catches wrong identities remains unverified at classroom scale.

The current evidence is limited to a four-face archival classroom test, a five-face modern group test, and prior wide-shot detection failures. A real 30+ person back-of-room classroom photograph must be tested before finalizing the production review-queue safety claim. A top-1/top-2 distance margin is a possible future mitigation, but no production margin rule or threshold change has been made.
